# Chain notes

Quick notes on chains commonly tracked with sms_tool.

## Ethereum

- Largest L1 ecosystem; most airdrops target it.
- Watch for mainnet launches and protocol retro-drops.

## Solana

- Fast, cheap; ecosystem rewards active users of new protocols.

## Arbitrum / Optimism

- L2 rollups; periodic incentive programs for bridging and usage.

## Base

- Coinbase-backed L2; growing app ecosystem.

## BNB Chain

- High-volume consumer apps; regular campaign-style rewards.

## TON

- Telegram-linked; tap-to-earn games often airdrop TON to players.

## Sui

- Move-based L1; frequent testnet and devnet incentive rounds.
